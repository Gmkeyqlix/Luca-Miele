MO-CAP / ROBOTIC ARM MOVE 
Write me a text prompt for an AI video model that has robotic arm / motion control style rig camera movement, characterised by fast lateral passes, snappy accelerations but no shake. Ensure the character’s face and outfit remain stable and artefact free.

Start with a kneeling woman centered in frame. Execute a rapid sequence of motion-control moves with extreme, snappy accelerations and clean deceleration beats: (1) instant micro snap left, then micro snap right (two quick lateral jolts) while keeping her face centered via pan compensation; (2) a very fast left-to-right lateral pass across her with a hard stop; (3) immediate crash push-in to an extreme close-up of the face; (4) instant pull-back to the original distance; (5) immediate whip pan to re-center after a slight intentional overshoot; (6) a fast right-to-left return pass with a second hard stop; (7) final micro push-in punch to the face and snap back to the exact starting centered framing. No circular motion around the subject. Robotic arm / motion control, spline-keyframed, zero shake/jitter, level horizon. Stable face throughout, outfit artefact-free.

TILT 
Smooth vertical tilt upward at constant speed. Tripod-like stability. No pan, no roll, no dolly.

DOLLY IN / PUSH IN 
Camera dollies forward in a straight line toward the frame centre. Smooth stabilised motion. No zoom.

HANDHELD  
Frantic handheld camera movement with natural micro-shake and imperfect stabilisation. Responsive, slightly uneven translation. No intentional zoom.

TRACKING  
Tracking camera shot, have the camera translate forward at a steady running pace, matching the moving subject’s speed. Stabilised tracking. No zoom.

CRANE / JIB UP  
Camera rises vertically upward smoothly (boom up), maintaining stable horizon. No pan, no tilt, no zoom.

WHIP PAN 
Extremely fast horizontal pan with heavy motion blur, then a sharp settle into a stable frame. No translation, no zoom.

ORBIT / 360 WRAP
Full 360-degree stabilised orbit around the subject at constant radius and speed, maintaining consistent framing. No zoom.
